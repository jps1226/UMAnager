⚗️ Auto-Pick Formula

Every horse gets a total score built from three branches. The Risk Slider blends them:

Risk 0 → 100% Odds + 0% Form + 0% Pedigree (follow the favourite) Risk 50 → 50% Odds + 50% Form + 50% Pedigree Risk 100 → 0% Odds + 100% Form + 100% Pedigree (ignore the market entirely)

The weights below control how much each sub-signal contributes within its branch — they don't change the blend ratio, they change how loud each voice is before the blend happens. The ⓘ popover on any horse row shows the exact per-branch breakdown for that runner.

📊 Odds Branch active at low Risk — the market's collective intelligence
Odds Cap Score = Cap ÷ odds. A 1.0 horse earns the full Cap; a 2.5 horse earns Cap ÷ 2.5; a 10.0 longshot earns Cap ÷ 10. Default 100 keeps the Odds branch on the same point scale as Form, so neither dominates unfairly at Risk 50. Raise it to make favourites even stickier at low Risk.
100
📈 Form Branch scales in at higher Risk — who has actually been running well
Career Win Rate Scale Career (wins ÷ starts) × this scale. A 25% winner scores 25 pts; a 50% winner scores 50. Default 100 matches the Odds Cap so both branches carry equal headroom — the Risk slider then tilts the blend, not an imbalanced scale.
100
Freshness Bonus (pts / start) Added for every start below Breakeven; subtracted for every start above it. Compensates for the JRA market systematically under-pricing lightly-raced horses who haven't accumulated enough public form. Default 3 — at Breakeven 10, a true debut horse earns +30, a 20-start campaigner loses −30. Set to 0 to turn off.
3
Freshness Breakeven (starts) The starts count where the freshness adjustment is exactly zero — neither bonus nor penalty. Horses below this are "lightly raced"; above are "seasoned." Default 10 is roughly two seasons of light campaigning. Raise it if JRA's maiden/young-horse market feels efficient in your experience.
10
Recent Form Scale Multiplier on the last-3 recency score. Finishes are weighted 0.5 / 0.3 / 0.2 (most-recent first); each position converts via f(pos) = 1/pos for top-5, zero outside. A horse that ran 1st–1st–2nd scores ≈ 0.90 raw → 72 pts at default. Default 80 — slightly below Win Rate Scale because 3 races is noisier than a full career, but still the strongest single predictor we have.
80
Jockey Weight Applied to the shrinkage-adjusted A/E: ae_eff = 1 + (min(ae,2.0)−1)·n/(n+25). At n=10 the raw A/E is down-weighted to 29% credibility; at n=100 it's 80%. This prevents small-sample outliers from dominating. Below 30 starts the cell shows — and contributes nothing. Default 20 — equal weight to trainerWeight after the shrinkage fix.
40
Trainer Weight Same A/E logic as Jockey, rolling 180-day window (trainers need more samples). Below 20 starts contributes nothing. Default 20 — half of Jockey. Trainers influence conditioning and race selection, but on a given race day the jockey's skill in-run is more directly causal. The longer window also means the signal is "staler" by nature.
20
🧬 Pedigree Branch scales in at higher Risk — bloodline fit for conditions
Pedigree Scale Multiplier on your tracked-bloodline score — points from horses whose Sire, Dam, or BMS appears in your FAV / Watchlist pedigree lists. Default 30 — deliberately modest. Pedigree is a weak per-race predictor compared to form; it's best used as a tiebreaker between otherwise equal horses, not as a primary signal. Raise it only if you find your pedigree lists are tightly curated.
30
Sire Fit Weight The sire's historical win% on this exact race's surface + distance bucket (Sprint ≤1400 / Mile / Middle / Long), scaled here. A sire at 20% win rate on turf/mile adds 0.20 × weight pts. Requires ≥10 progeny starts in the bucket — below that, nothing is added. Default 10 — pure tiebreaker. Surface-fit is a real but small edge; keeping the weight low prevents one freak sire from dominating.
10
💴 Bet Sizing used for the Voting tab payout estimates — match your real OrePro stakes
Win Stake (¥) ¥ on the single ◎ Win ticket. Often higher than the per-combo Q/T stakes since you're buying one ticket, not a box.
Quinella Stake (¥ / combo) ¥ per combination in the Q Box. Total Q spend = this × C(marks, 2) — e.g. 4 marks = 6 combos.
Trio Stake (¥ / combo) ¥ per combination in the T Box. Total T spend = this × C(marks, 3) — e.g. 4 marks = 4 combos.
